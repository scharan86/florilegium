# Artificial Intelligence
>[!definition]
> Artificial intelligence is the capability of **computer systems** to perform tasks typically associated with **human intelligence** such as learning, reasoning, problem solving, perception and decision making. 

- It's a branch of [[Computer Science|computer science]] that deals with simulation of intelligent behavior in computers.
