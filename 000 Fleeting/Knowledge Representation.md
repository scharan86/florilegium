>[!Definition]
> KRR is a field of [[Artificial Intelligence|artificial intelligence]] to represent information about the world in a format that computer systems can use to complex tasks. 

- KRR is the backbone of of [[Symbolic AI]]
- Most modern systems rarely use KRR in isolation to handle complex tasks. 
- [[LLMs]] use KRR ([[Knowledge Graphs]]) to ground them in facts and prevent hallucinations. 
- KRR is also used for **explainability** in neural networks as it provides trace of logic humans can understand. 
- KRR uses [[Symbolic Representation|symbolic representation]] to encode what is true about a domain.
