
| Aspect                        | Univariate EDA                                            | Bivariate EDA                                                            | Multivariate EDA                                                                         |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**                | Analysis of a single variable in isolation                | Analysis of the relationship between two variables                       | Analysis of three or more variables simultaneously                                       |
| **Goal**                      | Understand distribution, central tendency, spread         | Understand association, correlation, or dependency between two variables | Understand complex interactions, patterns, and dependencies across many variables        |
| **Example**                   | Distribution of `Age` in a dataset                        | Relationship between `Age` and `Salary`                                  | Interaction of `Age`, `Salary`, `Experience`, and `Department` together                  |
| **Numerical Methods**         | Mean, median, mode, std dev, variance, skewness, kurtosis | Pearson/Spearman correlation, covariance, chi-square test                | PCA, factor analysis, correlation matrix, VIF                                            |
| **Visual Methods**            | Histogram, boxplot, KDE plot, bar chart, pie chart        | Scatter plot, line plot, grouped bar chart, heatmap (2 vars)             | Pair plot, heatmap (full), 3D scatter, parallel coordinates, facet grid                  |
| **Example Plot**              | Histogram of `Income`                                     | Scatter plot of `Height` vs `Weight`                                     | Pair plot of `Sepal Length`, `Sepal Width`, `Petal Length`, `Petal Width` (Iris dataset) |
| **Outlier Detection**         | Boxplot, z-score on single column                         | Scatter plot clusters, residuals                                         | Mahalanobis distance, isolation forest across features                                   |
| **Distribution Check**        | Normality test (Shapiro-Wilk) on one feature              | Joint distribution of two features                                       | Multivariate normality tests, covariance structure                                       |
| **Categorical Handling**      | Frequency table, bar chart of one category column         | Cross-tabulation (contingency table) of two categorical vars             | Multi-way contingency tables, mosaic plots                                               |
| **Dimensionality**            | 1D                                                        | 2D                                                                       | 3D and beyond (often reduced for visualization)                                          |
| **Complexity**                | Low                                                       | Moderate                                                                 | High                                                                                     |
| **Common Libraries (Python)** | `pandas.Series.describe()`, `seaborn.histplot()`          | `seaborn.scatterplot()`, `df.corr()`                                     | `seaborn.pairplot()`, `sklearn.decomposition.PCA`                                        |

Key distinction to remember: univariate tells you _what_ a variable looks like, bivariate tells you _how_ two variables relate, and multivariate tells you _how the system as a whole behaves_ — including confounding effects that would be invisible in lower-dimensional analysis.
