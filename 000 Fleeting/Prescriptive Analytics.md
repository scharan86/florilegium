>[!Definition]
>Prescriptive analytics includes predicting the outcome and recommending the actions to take. 

- It's the most advanced form of data analytics. 
- Carried out after [[Predictive Analytics]].

e.g. Recommending the optimal discount offer to high-risk customer to prevent churn.
