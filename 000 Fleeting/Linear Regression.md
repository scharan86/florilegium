>[!Definition]
> It's the simplest and the most foundational [[regression]] model. It assumes that the relationship between input ($x$) and output ($y$) can be **approximated** by a ==linear function==, which is a straight line in case of one input or a [[Hyperplane|hyperplane]] in higher dimensions. 
> 
> It also a [[Statistical Model|statistical model]], [[Geometric Object|geometric object]], and a [[Computational Procedure|computational procedure]] at the same time.
