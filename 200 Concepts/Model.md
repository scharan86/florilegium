>[!Definition] 
> A model is a mathematical and computational framework that can be trained to recognize patterns, predict outcomes or make decision based on available data.

`Pattern Recognition | Outcome Prediction | Decision Making`

- It takes some input, processes the input through a mathematical framework, produces output that approximate the pattern observed in data.
## Intuition
A model is essentially mathematical function that simulates **superficial understanding** ("models")  of  a domain well enough to describe or predict something in that domain. 

*It approximates a the relationship between variables in a domain to describe or predict something*. 
## AI Models
- In [[Artificial Intelligence|artificial intelligence]], models are trained (adjust model parameters) on a [[Training Dataset|training dataset]].
- A [[Validation Dataset|validation set]] is used to tune [[Hyperparameters]] and select the best model.
- They are tested using a [[Test Dataset| test dataset or test set]] to  estimate the  extent of [[Generalization|generalization]] (generalization performance) on unseen data.
