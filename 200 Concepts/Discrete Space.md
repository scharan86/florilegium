>[!Definition]
>A *discrete space* is a space made up of separable and countable [[states]] and not a continuous range.

e.g. **8 Puzzle**: where the tile arrangements are separate [[Atomic|atomic]] states that can be counted. Total states for 8 Puzzle $$\frac{9!}{2} = 181,440$$
- A [[State Space Search|state space search]] requires the problems to have discrete states.
