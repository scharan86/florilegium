- It's an abstraction over **raw domain-knowledge**. 
- It hides irrelevant details. i.e. we generally don't care about how a rule is stored. 
- It converts the raw domain-knowledge into a format [[Inference Engine|inference engine]] can manipulate. 

>[!question]- Why do we need different methods to represent knowledge? 
>Knowledge is **heterogeneous**, it exists in different modalities such as procedural, declarative, or structural. Therefore, a knowledge engineer must select a knowledge representation schema that suits the domain's [[Ontology|ontology]]
