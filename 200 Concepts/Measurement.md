>[!Definition]
>It's the standard process by which a value is assigned to a variable.

e.g. Temperature (real-world)  measured using a thermometer (instrument) gives the reading $28^.$ C (value).

- If the thermometers were calibrated differently, then the data becomes inconsistent and unreliable.
## Intuition
It maps real world property to a numerical representation (number).

>[!question] What operations on a specific data are meaningful? 
>All numbers are not equal in the sense that if we label multiple categories with 1,2,3, etc. Those numbers don't have any arithmetic value. Therefore, the legitimacy of operations depends on the kind of information that the data carries.
## Levels of Data Measurement
==The levels of measurements tells you what operations produce meaningful insight on the given data.==
1. [[Nominal Data]] (No arithmetic operation)
2. [[Ordinal Data]] (No arithmetic operation)
3. [[Interval Data]] (can add, subtract but no multiplication)
4. [[Ratio Data]] (all arithmetic operations)
