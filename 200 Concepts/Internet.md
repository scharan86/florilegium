>[!Definition]
> It refers to the global system of interconnected computer networks that uses the [[TCP/IP]] network model to facilitate communication between networks and devices.

- It's the largest distributed [[Computer Networks|network]] in the world. 
- Internet is the transport substrate on which several [[Application Layer|app-layer]] protocols run.
- It's a global [[Packet Switched Network|packet-switched network]] of networks built on top of [[IP]] and [[TCP]] / [[UDP]].
