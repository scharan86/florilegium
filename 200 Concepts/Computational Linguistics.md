>[!definition] Computational Linguistics
>It's an interdisciplinary field that is concerned with the computational modeling of natural language. It draws from [[Linguistics]], [[Computer Science]], [[Artificial Intelligence]]

- It tries to build machines that can interpret or produce human language.
- To achieve that the machine needs two things:
	1. A formal description of language structure: [[Formal Grammars]]
	2. A procedure that uses that description to analyze sentences: [[Parsing]]
- Therefore, grammars define the rules of the language and parsing is the algorithm that applies those rules to a sentence.
## Two Primary Objectives of Computational Linguistics
1. **Analysis**: Figuring out the structure and meaning of the sentence.  (i.e. what does this shit mean?)
2. **Generation**: Given a meaning or intent, *produce fluent language*. (i.e, how do i say it?)
## Levels of language structure studied in CL
1. [[Morphology]]
2. [[Syntax]]
3. [[Semantics]]
4. [[Pragmatics]]
