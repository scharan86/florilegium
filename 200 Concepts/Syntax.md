In linguistics, syntax studies the grammatical structure of sentences. It tries to answer how words are arranged to form a valid sentence.

- Structure refers to the rules that govern how words are combined into phrases and phrases into sentences.
- [[Generative Grammar]] is a widely accepted theory on how humans generate these structures.

>[!example]
>> "Colorless green ideas sleep furiously" — syntactically perfect, semantically nonsensical. "Ideas green colorless furiously sleep" — syntactically broken.
