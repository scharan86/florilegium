>[!note] Ontology
>It's a branch of philosophy that studies the nature of being and existence, focusing on what entities exist and how they're categorized (represented).

- In simpler terms, it is concerned with the types of thing that make up reality and how they relate to eachother.
- Types, properties and interrelationships of the entities in a specific domain.
