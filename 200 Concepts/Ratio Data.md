>[!Definition]
> It is the highest form of measurement, everything from [[Interval Data|interval data]] applies and zero genuinely means absence of the quantity.
> 

## Properties
1. All interval properties hold
2. True zero exists
3. Multiplication and division hold

>[!example]
>- Height, weight, distance — 0 cm means no height
> - Income: someone earning ₹0 has no income
> - Reaction time in milliseconds
 >- Number of training epochs, model accuracy (0% to 100%)
> - Pixel intensity values (0 = no light)
> - Age
