>[!Definition]
> Data that describes qualities, categories or characteristics.

## Intuition
Qualitative data describes the nature of something more than its quantity or mathematical utility. The numbers assigned to qualitative data don't represent **measurable magnitude**. Therefore, arithmetic operations aren't meaningful.
