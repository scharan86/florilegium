>[!important] Can't we just do `tokens = sentence.split(" ")` and call it a day?
>The problem of tokenization might seem simple at first glance. However, natural languages do not always clearly indicate where words begin or end. As a result, tokenization becomes non-trivial: a tokenizer must correctly handle ambiguous boundaries caused by phenomena such as contractions, punctuation, hyphenation, and morphologically complex words in order to produce valid tokens.
