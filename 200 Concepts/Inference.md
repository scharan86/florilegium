>[!definition]
>*Inference* is the process of deriving new facts or conclusion from known facts and rules using a reasoning mechanism.

 - In [[Artificial Intelligence|AI]] systems like [[Expert Systems|expert systems]], the reasoning mechanism is an [[Inference Engine|inference engine]]
- An AI system performs inference whenever it:
	1. Predicts something
	2. Deduces something
	3. Estimates something
	4. Reasons about something
	   All of these involve inferring hidden information from observed data.
- In [[Symbolic AI]], inference means **deriving logical conclusions** from premises.
## Components in inference
1. **Knowledge**: Rules or logical statements.
2. **Facts**: Statements known to be true in the current situation.
3. **Reasoning Mechanism**: Procedure that applies rules to facts to derive new knowledge. In [[Expert Systems| expert systems]], this mechanism is generally a [[Production Systems In AI| production system]].
